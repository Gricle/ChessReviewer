Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Chess Reviewer ("we", "the app") is a free, browser-based chess game analysis tool. This policy explains what data is processed when you use it, and your choices. We aim to collect as little as possible — the chess analysis itself runs entirely on your own device.

Analysis stays in your browser

When you paste a PGN or import a game, the Stockfish engine runs locally in your browser using WebAssembly. Your game moves are analyzed on your device and are not uploaded to any server by the analysis process.

Optional account & cloud sync

If you choose to sign in, your saved reviews are stored in a Supabase database tied to your account, protected by row-level security so only you can read your own data. Signing in is entirely optional; the app is fully functional in guest mode with no account.

Importing from Chess.com and Lichess

When you enter a username to import games, your browser requests that user's public games directly from the Chess.com and Lichess public APIs. Those requests are subject to the respective platforms' privacy policies.

Advertising & cookies

We may display ads served by Google AdSense to keep Chess Reviewer free. When ads are enabled and you consent:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • For more on how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

We use Google Consent Mode. Ads only load after you accept via the consent banner. If you reject, advertising and analytics storage remain disabled. You can change your choice by clearing this site's data in your browser (the consent preference is stored under chessreviewer.consent).

Local storage

We use your browser's local storage for preferences such as your chosen language, sound settings, and consent decision. This data never leaves your device.

Children

Chess Reviewer is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the date above.
